# Vision Simulator 1.1.0 — release notes

> Released 2026-10-29 as tag `v1.1.0` (`feature/rect-spot` merged into `main`).

## What's new

### + Spot (rectangle)

You can now add a **rectangle** to the picture, as well as the oval.

- In the Regions tab, tap **+ Spot (rectangle)**, then tap the picture where you want it. The rectangle
  appears there.
- It works like the oval spot you already know, and it stays a rectangle:
  - **White dot** (middle): move it.
  - **Yellow dots** (middle of each edge): make it wider or taller.
  - **Blue dot**: turn it. It snaps to neat angles, like the oval.
  - **Arrow keys** move it a little at a time. Hold Shift for bigger steps.
- **Soft edge:** the edge can fade out gently. The corners are rounded and fade evenly, so there is no hard
  line or crease.
- **All effects work on a rectangle**, including **Size change** (make things look bigger or smaller).
- It appears in the list as "Rectangle 1", "Rectangle 2", and so on. Rename, hide, duplicate, reorder,
  delete, undo and redo, save and load, export and the timeline all work as they do for the oval.

Handy for things that really are rectangles: a patch of text, a screen, a door, a window.

## Important: update every device before sharing a profile with a rectangle

A saved profile that **contains a rectangle** cannot be opened by the older version 1.0.x app. The old app
shows the message **"unknown shape type"** and refuses the profile.

Before you use or share rectangle profiles:

1. **Update the app on every device** you use (phone, tablet, laptop). Reload the page, or reinstall the
   app if you installed it to the home screen.
2. Do this **before** you open a rectangle profile on that device, or send one to your doctor.

Profiles **without** a rectangle are not affected. They open in every version, old and new.
